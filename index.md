### Hi, I'm Hayeon Lee

I am a PhD candidate at Kyung Hee University working at the intersection of digital health, medical informatics, medical AI, biomedical engineering, and mathematical modelling. My research focuses on using large-scale health data and computational methods to understand disease burden, treatment outcomes, and population-level health risks.

I enjoy working across clinical, epidemiological, and engineering perspectives, and I value research that is rigorous, collaborative, and useful beyond a single discipline. Outside research, I play piano and drums, practice yoga, and enjoy watching Formula 1.
